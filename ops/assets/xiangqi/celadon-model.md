# Celadon model source

An original contemporary Xiangqi collection: pale jade-grey glazed ceramic discs with carved red or charcoal characters, a recessed circular face line, a softly rolled rim, stepped shoulder and an undercut unglazed foot. This is a contemporary interpretation, not a replica of a museum object, a named potter's work or a claim that a particular historical set used this colour scheme.

The [Palace Museum Journal, 2024 issue 11, p.28](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2024/11/26/u67458b7518e3a.pdf) documents round ceramic Xiangqi discs with incised perimeter grooves, glazed sides and written character faces. The [British Museum's Chinese porcelain chess pieces](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_1998-0220-4) also support the cylindrical ceramic form. [UNESCO's account of Longquan celadon](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-firing-technology-of-longquan-celadon-00205) supplies the glaze craft context. These references inform the material and construction; the precise profile, dimensions, shaders and adapted lettering below are original to this collection.

The app uses 53 mm intersection spacing. Each disc has a 44 mm maximum diameter and a 12.8 mm maximum height, leaving 9 mm between adjacent pieces. The application's 2 mm board offset brings its highest point to 14.8 mm, below the existing 16 mm camera envelope. This is an app presentation scale, not an assertion of official tournament dimensions. Red and black characters remain legible on a shared pale body, consistent with the [World Xiangqi Federation's equipment guidance](https://www.wxf-xiangqi.org/images/wxf-rules/2018_World_XiangQi_Rules_English2018.pdf).

Fourteen semantic roots preserve the existing native Xiangqi contract. `light` is red; `dark` is black. Each root is named `{side}_{role}`, with roles `general`, `advisor`, `elephant`, `horse`, `chariot`, `cannon`, `soldier`. Their corresponding red faces are 帥, 仕, 相, 傌, 俥, 炮, 兵; black faces are 將, 士, 象, 馬, 車, 砲, 卒. There is no promotion face or underside lettering. Root extras record collection, owner, role and frontGlyph; geometry carries the actual shape and colour.

The builder authors the physical ceramic body, a blind 0.4 mm character cut with surviving walls and floor, and a separate thin inset pigment layer. The face lies 12.3 mm above the contact plane; recess floors lie at 11.9 mm and pigment spans 11.908–11.935 mm. The annular channel has the same floor, with its unsoftened bottom profile between radii 18.15 and 18.60 mm and shaped rising walls. Its pigment ring is inset from both walls. The foot's unsoftened contact profile spans radii 18.1–18.7 mm; the central underside is recessed by 1.1 mm. The common blank receives a 0.045 mm edge finish before carving. A separate fine bevel, limited to the character edges, softens the cut lip without letting a glyph's tight features change the shared exterior. No colour decal substitutes for the engraved body surface.

The licensed letter source is **Noto Serif CJK TC Bold**, copyright 2017–2024 Adobe, under SIL OFL 1.1; see [FONT-LICENSE.txt](../intersection/FONT-LICENSE.txt). The pinned upstream file is [NotoSerifCJKtc-Bold.otf](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/blob/main/Serif/OTF/TraditionalChinese/NotoSerifCJKtc-Bold.otf), SHA-256 `a4441a76dbf56719600c5dcbd5b5e5a068a20944cc41c959487a657133576ee6`. A restrained 10-font-unit silhouette expansion strengthens the fine strokes; contours are united before extrusion to preserve open counters and remove overlapping stroke surfaces. The font, editable text curves and converted meshes are retained in the Blender source. Individually generated 2D references guide colour and silhouette; their brushwork is not represented as an exact match to the verified 3D Noto glyphs.

Original glaze and biscuit shaders bake to embedded colour, roughness and tangent-space normal maps. Bake planes match the 52 mm physical UV scale, preserving restrained microtexture through the normal-map encoding. Colour intermediates use JPEG; roughness and normal intermediates use lossless PNG. Procedural nodes and packed images remain editable. Vermilion and charcoal pigments use separate PBR materials with `ink` in their names so the app can preserve ownership during finish changes. No remote texture or font loads are required at runtime.

Rebuild from the repository root using Blender 5.2:

```
blender -b -t 4 --python ops/assets/xiangqi/build_celadon.py
```

The pinned font must exist at `output/playwright/intersection-font/NotoSerifCJKtc-Bold.otf`. The outline pipeline shares the isolated builder dependencies at `output/atelier/hori/vendor`: use Blender's bundled Python to install `fonttools==4.66.0` and `shapely==2.1.2` there. These dependencies are not application dependencies. Default rebuilds create fresh maps. Add `-- --prototype` for a scratch red/black general proof, close-up, side profile and approximately 40-pixel disc-width render. Full builds also render an approximately 20-pixel disc-width proof. `--stage` writes the complete collection to ignored scratch paths; `--reuse-bakes` is only suitable when material source is unchanged. Intermediate images and geometry audit records live in `output/atelier/celadon`.

Delivery paths are `public/assets/xiangqi/celadon.glb`, `ops/assets/xiangqi/celadon.blend` and `ops/assets/xiangqi/celadon-preview.png`. The GLB retains separate ceramic, character and ring meshes to make the physical construction reviewable. Identical exterior ceramic triangles are shared between semantic roots; each distinct carved face retains its original triangles. Splitting preserves corner normals and UVs and adds no hidden cap, duplicate surface or lower-detail substitute. Exact attribute digests prevent surfaces with different attributes from being shared accidentally. The two owners also reuse their identical pigment-ring meshes. This reduces download duplication while preserving every assembled piece's surface.

The builder enforces contact, diameter and height envelopes, and a conservative 25,000-triangle emergency ceiling per root; the intended production target is below 20,000 triangles per root and 7 MiB for the collection.

The validated delivery contains 14 semantic roots, four material roles and six embedded PBR images. It is 7,069,776 bytes (6.74 MiB), with 11,366–17,520 assembled triangles per piece. All pieces remain 44 mm across and 12.8 mm high. One exterior geometry and two owner-coloured ring geometries are shared; the fourteen carved faces remain distinct. The staged asset suite passed 60 checks, including actual ray intersections through the character and ring recesses, contact/underside geometry, bounds, native glyph identities, UVs, material-map variation, and the companion generated images. The studio preview and approximately 20-pixel disc-width proof were inspected; actual application lighting and camera views are separate integration checks.
