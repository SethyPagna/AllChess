# Carved Shogi collection

Shogi and Mini Shogi gain a second coordinated piece collection, Carved (`hori`), alongside Printed. Fifteen individually generated transparent masters cover both king identities, seven other front faces and six promotions. Both owners share each native face except 玉/王; tile direction conveys ownership. Role sizes keep a modest king-to-pawn hierarchy. Every prompt, reference and generation identifier is retained in `ops/assets/shogi/hori/`.

The portable 28-root GLB uses genuinely carved front and reverse glyphs, inset sumi/vermilion lacquer, fine bevels, graded wedge proportions and embedded longitudinal/endgrain wood maps. Original shader nodes, strengthened licensed Noto Serif outlines and converted meshes remain in the packed Blender source. The models are independently authored from the generated material/silhouette references; lettering is coordinated rather than reconstructed exactly. Provenance and reproduction details live in `ops/assets/shogi/hori-model.md`.

A separately generated kaya-style colour image spans the Wood board continuously in both 2D and 3D, with the same colour on the physical case and hand stands. Grid geometry, stars, move markers, state highlights and lighting remain live. Other board colours stay independently selectable. Failed optional board texture requests retain the original playable wooden surface, and all new public assets enter the verified offline pack. Multi-colour production review found that the old Japanese case top and playable tiles were coplanar, producing severe depth-buffer striping. The case now ends 2 mm below the tile tops, with its lower bound and supporting legs unchanged.

Printed remains the default for both games. Collection, 2D style, board colour, 3D finish and view use separate per-game preferences. Legacy Clear/Letters choices migrate without selecting the new collection accidentally. Captured icons, promotion choices and move history receive the selected artwork. Corrected board/hand facing follows board rotation across all Shogi styles. Native drag previews copy the source icon's computed transform so moving the clone outside its tray does not reverse it. Shogi Letters now has a fixed pale surface and dark ink in both app themes, including its promotion faces and previews; Artwork and Clear remain unchanged.

Asset verification inspects actual geometry, portable maps, root identities, paired reverse glyphs, physical recess depths, alpha images and generation provenance. Prototype review strengthened fragile font hairlines. The first full asset run identified flat normal maps caused by a bake plane at the wrong physical scale. Corrected fresh bakes now contain restrained directional surface relief. All 228 geometry payloads and 28 root transforms remain unchanged by the material correction. The 75 focused asset checks pass.

Playing-size review then lowered the wood's linear colour by 12% so far-rank points separate more clearly from the board at 320px. Geometry, root transforms, both lacquer materials and all four normal/roughness images remain byte-identical to the checked carving model; only the two colour images changed.

The final GLB is 7,057,988 bytes, with six embedded maps and four materials. Its largest piece has 9,656 triangles and a height of 11.2814 mm, below both the 12 mm hand-stack spacing and 16 mm board camera envelope. The source Blender file is 24,311,364 bytes and packs the licensed font and textures.

The full default suite passes 851 tests across 83 files. Lint and TypeScript pass. An isolated real-browser audit physically starts native mouse drags for 24 owner/tray/style cases across both games; the DOM handed to `setDragImage` keeps the source's facing direction and label without changing the source or transforming its wrapper. The OS-rendered bitmap itself was not captured.

Production validation covers 20 preference scenarios and 48 mobile control combinations, including 96 native drag handoffs, 44px targets, keyboard focus, board rotation and per-game isolation. Eight matching-build Light/Dark checks at 320/390px measure at least 10.79:1 normal-letter contrast and 6.19:1 promoted-letter contrast. Clear and Artwork remain unchanged by that correction.

Four legal 3D gameplay scenarios exercise both games at 320/1440px: both owners capture, promote and drop, wrong-owner and occupied drops are rejected, history hands are read-only, undo/redo restores exact state, and camera controls preserve the position. Twelve additional fixture views inspect selected three-tile captured stacks from default, side and low angles. The final appearance sweep covers both games, both collections, five colours and three widths: 60 combinations with 146 captures including finishes and orbit samples. There are no runtime exceptions, failed required assets, overflow or unintended moves. Playing-size review confirms the physical case striping is resolved and the darker Carved faces remain readable. A remaining subpixel grid discontinuity prompted a separate Japanese-grid correction and focused follow-up.

Missing GLB, forced WebGL context loss, collection remount and optional board-image failure all retain both owners' promoted pieces and captured hands. A fresh persistent browser profile downloads the final 7,057,988-byte model and sixteen images, closes fully, then restarts at `/offline` with networking already disabled. Both games restore independent collection/view/colour/finish choices; all fifteen sprites and the board image decode, the cached GLB exposes all 28 roots, and both owners' captures, promotions, drops, undo/redo and camera controls pass offline without errors or failed requests.

The production build generates 209 pages and 159 public offline assets (74.4 MiB). Evidence is recorded in `output/playwright/hori/`. This delivery does not establish deployed multiplayer or physical-device performance.

The Japanese grid uses a depth-tested ink plane at the original bar-top height, with derivative-based edge coverage. It retains the physical 0.6 mm stroke and a restrained 0.7 CSS-pixel minimum, adjusted for the renderer's pixel ratio. Pieces and raised markers occlude it normally; camera movement requires no per-frame CPU projections. Other game families retain their existing grid geometry. The plane and shader are disposed with the scene. Independent visual review accepts default, side and low-angle views at both 320 and 1440px: lines stay continuous on the surface and disappear behind pieces without an overlay artifact. The focused follow-up passes 25 cases and 75 captures: both games, both collections, Wood/Jade and 320/390/1440px, plus a 2× pixel-density phone case. Camera stages remain fixed with no overflow, failed required assets, runtime exceptions or changed game state.

Eight further collection/colour combinations produce no console errors or shader warnings. Targeted lint, TypeScript and a fresh production build pass after the grid change. The complete cold-restart sequence is repeated on that exact build using a second fresh profile: both games, all assets, preferences, promotions, drops, history and camera controls pass with networking disabled before launch.

The all-app goal remains active. Shogi still needs a third independently designed coordinated collection, and Printed retains vector 2D art. Most other native families still require further collections and richer assets. Recoloured finishes do not count as independent sets.
